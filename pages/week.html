Needs attention

    How these numbers are counted

    Sends are emails, not people. The same person mailed twice counts twice.

    Deliveries are sends minus hard and soft bounces. The click rate uses people who clicked, divided by deliveries.

    Campaign counters are lifetime totals grouped by the date the campaign went out. A campaign sent yesterday has had less time to collect opens and clicks than one sent last week, which is what “results at the same age” is for.

    Opens and clicks can include automated scanning by mail providers.
